Clinical protocol

    Why Haloscan?

    Reese's Law fixed prevention — child-proof battery compartments. It didn't fix diagnosis. When a disc appears on X-ray, clinicians have ~2 hours if it's a battery in the esophagus.
    • Stacked coins mimic the double halo sign on AP films
    • Emory 2020 reported a YOLOv2 model on real radiographs. HaloScan numbers are synthetic and are not a comparison.
    • Haloscan: CV halo + DualViewNet fusion + Grad-CAM explainability
    • Weights trained on Kaggle CPU · runs fully offline
